Our process

How Your New Roof Goes On

A full replacement, layer by layer, shown with photos from L.I.N.Y. jobs. Scroll through it, then ask Ryan how it applies to your house.

01/06Tear-off
STEP 01

Tear-off, down to the deck

The old shingles and underlayment come off so the roof deck can be seen and checked, not covered over.

STEP 02

Deck check & repairs

Soft or damaged boards get cut out and replaced, so the new roof sits on solid wood.

STEP 03

Underlayment & ice protection

A waterproof layer goes down over the whole deck, with extra protection at the edges and valleys where water collects.

STEP 04

Flashing that keeps leaks out

Skylights, chimneys and walls get new flashing, the spots most leaks start from.

STEP 05

New shingles

Shingles are laid in straight, tight courses in the color you picked, from the eaves up to the ridge.

STEP 06

Ridge, vents & cleanup

Ridge caps and vents finish the roof, then the property gets cleaned up before the crew leaves.

General steps of a full replacement. Every roof is different; Ryan will walk you through yours at the estimate.
